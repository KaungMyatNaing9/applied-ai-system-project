# Pet Care Knowledge Base

## Medication
Medication tasks should be treated as high priority because missed or delayed doses can affect a pet's routine. Owners should follow the veterinarian's instructions exactly and should contact a veterinarian before changing or doubling a dose after a missed medication task.

## Feeding
Feeding should happen on a consistent schedule whenever possible. If a pet suddenly refuses food, eats much less than usual, or vomits after meals, the owner should monitor closely and contact a veterinarian if the issue seems urgent or persistent.

## Walks
Walks help support exercise, enrichment, and bathroom breaks. Walk timing should avoid extreme heat, icy conditions, or unsafe weather, and shorter walks may be safer for older pets or pets recovering from illness.

## Grooming
Grooming is usually lower urgency than medication or feeding, but it still matters for coat health, comfort, and hygiene. Regular brushing, coat checks, and nail care can help prevent matting, skin irritation, and discomfort.

## Vet Appointments
Vet appointments should not be skipped when they involve follow-up care, vaccinations, or active symptoms. If an appointment is missed or a pet's condition seems to worsen, the owner should contact the veterinary clinic for next steps.

## Safety Warnings
Owners should pay attention to overlapping tasks, missed high-priority care, and signs that the daily plan is overloaded. Unsafe advice should never replace veterinary guidance, and urgent medical concerns should be escalated to a veterinarian immediately.

# Pet Care Knowledge Base

## Medication
Medication tasks should be treated as high priority. If a medication is missed, the owner should contact a veterinarian before doubling a dose.

## Feeding
Feeding should happen consistently. Sudden changes in appetite may be a health concern.

## Walks
Dogs usually benefit from regular walks. Walk timing should avoid extreme heat or unsafe weather.

## Grooming
Grooming is lower urgency than medication or feeding, but regular grooming helps prevent matting and skin issues.

## Vet Appointments
Vet appointments should not be skipped. If symptoms are severe, urgent care may be needed.
